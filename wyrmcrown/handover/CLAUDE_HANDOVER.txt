DRAGON WARS / WYRMCROWN — AUDIO INTEGRATION HANDOVER
7 October 2026

START FROM THIS VERSION
This project was copied from the user's supplied Desktop wyrmcrown-game
folder. Its newer mechanics and artwork were preserved, and the existing
recorded audio library was merged into that version. Use this as your base.
Keep the wyrmcrown and alien-strike folders together.

PLAYING
Double-click PLAY_GAME.cmd at the project root. Python 3 serves the project
locally and opens the correct game copy on an available port.
Alternatively: python wyrmcrown/tools/serve.py --port 8766
Game: http://127.0.0.1:8766/wyrmcrown/index.html
Audio review: http://127.0.0.1:8766/wyrmcrown/tools/audio-review.html
Opening index.html directly uses synthesized audio fallbacks.

LIBRARY
428 unique dialogue lines for eight locked, approved character voices.
443 dialogue takes include 15 superseded versions; runtime picks the latest.
125 SFX assets include 12 assembled 0.9-second meal variants and 15 ingredient
stems used to assemble those meals. 50 auditions and provider originals and
processing history are preserved.
Preserve MP3s and JSON sidecars. Regeneration can cost money and does not
guarantee identical results. Playing the game makes no provider API calls.

RUNTIME
data/dialogue.js defines line IDs, characters, events and text.
data/recordings.js is the exported asset and approved-cast manifest.
src/audio/recorded_audio.js extends AS.Audio's existing mixer, variation and
loops, using synthesized fallbacks on load failure.
src/audio/dialogue_director.js extends AS.Voices' existing queue, subtitles,
priorities, distance attenuation and browser-speech fallback.
Speech plays at rate 1 with the locked identity/settings. Music and effects
duck during speech. Urgent home warnings interrupt chatter. Rider/dragon
banter shares a 180–300-second interval and 90-second introductory quiet period.

HOOKS
Combat: breath inhale/ignite/bursts/release, four faction loops, rock attacks,
missed shots and large hits.
Feeding: sheep, cattle, goat/deer and large-prey meals, with occasional dragon
reactions. Meals play once at rate 1 without an extra swallow.
Troops: giant/troll movement, pain, deaths, melee and contextual idle sounds;
cart losses, nearby troop losses and raised-dead callouts.
Town/factions: loot, danger, lost livestock/buildings, elimination and victory.
Victory speech extends the ending display for the selected line.
Exploration: sites, borders, resources, pickups, spells, waygates, returning
home and roost recovery.
Warnings: health, mana, energy, hunger, storms, retreat and broken rival wards.
Pause, overlays and muting stop speech and pending replies. Held breath loops
switch to recordings when those assets finish loading.

OBJECTIVE REWARD DIALOGUE UPDATE
68 recorded capture_reward_<kind> lines cover all 17 objective kinds in all
four factions. Both wizard and dragon voices participate in each faction.
onCapture selects the specific benefit line for owned objectives, including
neutral captures. Site.loot calls onObjectiveReward for caves and ruins.
Treasure lines tell the player to collect coin pickups; ownership is not
assigned to hoards. Enemy captures do not congratulate the player.

Pending rewards are separate from the short-lived chatter queue. They play
at priority 65, below danger warnings, with a 90-second validity window.
Warnings can defer unsaid rewards, even when interrupting unrelated speech.
Lost ownership, expired hoards, mute, pause/stop and a new match cancel stale
announcements. Multiple captures are announced in order, without duplicate
same-owner events. Existing generic capture recordings are preserved.

tools/objective_dialogue.py is the repeatable source for the 68 new lines.
dialogue_catalog.py includes these when rebuilding. Unchanged production
lines retain their existing retake directions. Do not regenerate existing
audio just to rebuild the catalog.
For an explicit new recording batch: audio_generate.py dialogue --only
capture_reward_. The generator preserves existing takes and locked settings.

Read OBJECTIVE_REWARDS.txt for the active benefits behind the wording.
The supplied data declares wizardtower staff, castle crown and nest passive
bonuses, but AS.Sites.bonus has no gameplay callers. This dialogue update
preserves gameplay and does not promise those inactive stat bonuses.
Spell generators retain their 100/110-second cadence and only add charges
when the dragon holds no spell or the same spell, capped at three charges.

Follow-up checks:
- test_objective_dialogue.mjs covers all 68 combinations and reward lifecycle.
- test_objective_recordings.mjs checks real capture/loot callbacks, all eight
  recorded voices, original playback rate and matching subtitles in Edge.
- test_objective_transcripts.py checks cached transcripts and key quantities.
The 68 transcript checks passed; they do not certify acting or pronunciation.

SUPPLIED SOUNDTRACK UPDATE
All eight user-supplied WAVs were imported as stereo, 44.1 kHz, 192 kbps MP3s.
Soft Flowing Strings is both the opening theme and human_home. The Human
dragon fight track is human_fight; the other six files map to their named
realm and home/fight role. See MUSIC_HANDOVER.txt for the complete mapping.

index.html now loads src/audio/recorded_music.js instead of the shared
procedural music.js, plus data/music.js. The replacement retains the Music
attach/play/stop/setIntensity/stinger/update interface used by the game.
It streams through two HTML audio elements and MediaElementAudioSourceNodes
into the existing musicDuck/musicBus/master mixer. Compositions play at rate
1, fade between tracks and crossfade into another copy at the loop boundary.
No procedural musical layers or end stingers are scheduled by this game.
Victory/defeat sound effects stay on their existing effects bus.

realm_audio.js still settles borders for four seconds before changing music.
Neutral territory resolves to the player's realm. Combat uses the current
territory's fight track, with a nine-second hold after danger. Pause/overlays
soften the score, results return to the home theme, and menus restore Soft
Flowing Strings. screens.js explicitly requests the supplied opening theme
and no longer claims that all music is generated in code.

tools/prepare_music.py performs a measured two-pass loudness conversion and
fresh stereo/duration/peak verification. It makes no provider API calls.
Original supplied WAVs are preserved in the user's Downloads folder; the ZIP
contains the playable MP3s, metadata, checksums and repeatable import tool.
tools/test_music.mjs and test_music_browser.mjs cover the new music lifecycle.
tools/serve.py now supports single HTTP byte ranges for audio files, including
HEAD, suffix/open ranges, 416 responses and If-Range fallback. This enables
browser seeking and streaming; test_music_ranges.py verifies the exact bytes.
Recorded dialogue, objective announcements, sound effects and gameplay are
retained from the previous updated version.
Other supplied files, including newer art, scenery, hunting and flight code,
are preserved. The integration did not replace whole gameplay files with the
older audio-production game version.

PRODUCTION TOOLS
Decode audits need FFmpeg or imageio_ffmpeg. Generation/transcription tools
need their existing dependencies and ELEVENLABS_API_KEY from the environment.
The game does not need any production dependency or API key.
Casting choices were approved in the prior audio work. Automated checks verify
integrity and playback. A full human listening review of acting, pronunciation
and SFX remains pending. Original and revised takes are included for comparison.
