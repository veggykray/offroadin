DRAGON WARS — WYRMCROWN WITH RECORDED AUDIO

To play:
1. Extract the whole ZIP.
2. Open the wyrmcrown-voiced-game folder.
3. Double-click PLAY_GAME.cmd.
4. Keep its window open while playing.

Python 3 is required to run the launcher. It is already available on the
computer used for this integration. The launcher chooses an available local
port and opens the correct game copy in your default browser.

428 dialogue lines for eight characters and 125 recorded sound-effect assets
are included. Click once in the game to enable sound, then adjust Voices and
Sound Effects in Options if needed.

This version adds 68 recorded reward announcements covering all 17 objective
types. Your wizard or dragon explains the benefit when you capture an objective
or open a treasure hoard. Urgent warnings can delay the announcement.

The soundtrack now uses all eight music files supplied by you. Soft Flowing
Strings plays on the opening screens and in the human homeland. Each realm
has its own home and combat track, with fades between them. Music volume is
controlled in Options, and voices lower it briefly while speaking.

Keep the wyrmcrown and alien-strike folders together.

Give Claude this updated project as the starting version and ask it to read
CLAUDE_HANDOVER.txt. Keep the included audio files when making future updates.
