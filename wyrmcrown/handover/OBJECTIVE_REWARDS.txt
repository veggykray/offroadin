WYRMCROWN — REWARDS USED BY THE NEW CAPTURE DIALOGUE
7 October 2026

These describe active mechanics in this supplied game version. Gold income
is delivered by cart (or raven when no ground route exists), not credited as
a lump sum on capture. Regular owned sites also reveal their surroundings.

Gold Mine: gold production for delivery home. Rich mines produce more.
Village: taxes, +2 troop capacity, and ownership of the village herd.
Trade Post: gold caravans for the town.
Wizard Tower: magical defensive fire and map vision.
Magic Well: nearby dragon healing and mana recovery within 320 units.
Enchanted Grove: nearby dragon energy recovery within 320 units.
Mana Crystal: Lightning Storm charges on a 100-second timer.
Relic Site: Call the Host charges on a 110-second timer.
Abandoned Fort: four guards, arrow defences, +3 troop capacity and vision.
Old Castle: gold deliveries, four guards, strong defences, +4 troop capacity.
Bridge: 15% toll from rival gold carts crossing it and defensive arrows.
Shrine: nearby dragon healing within 320 units and wider map vision.
Cave: drops 220 gold as collectible coins. The hoard later respawns.
Dragon Eyrie: map vision around the eyrie.
Watchtower: wide map vision (1700-unit reveal radius).
Waygate: own at least two to travel between them with E while near a gate.
Ruins: drops 150 gold as coins, with a 60% chance of a power-up orb.

Crystal/relic charges are not granted immediately on capture. Their timer
adds a charge only when no different spell occupies the dragon's spell slot;
the same spell can stack to three charges. Hoards require coin collection,
and their sites are looted rather than permanently owned.
