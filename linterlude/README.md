# Linterlude: Chapter 1, The Lint Trap

A small Undertale-style RPG about a sock that gets sucked through the dryer's lint trap
and lands in the **Lintlands**, where everything lost in the wash ends up.

It is a single, dependency-free HTML file. Open `index.html` in a browser, or run
`npm run dev` and visit `/linterlude/`. On a phone, on-screen controls appear.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Arrows / WASD | Move (and move your SOLE in battle) |
| Z / Enter / Space | Confirm, talk, advance text |
| X / Shift | Cancel, fast-forward text, hold during the intro to skip it |
| C | Menu (items, stats) |
| M | Mute |

## What's in Chapter 1

- **Opening sequence**: a storybook intro about the ancient war of Left and Right socks, a
  hero who refuses to wake up, a 90-minute tumble dry, and a long fall past the TV remote.
  After that comes the title card and naming your sock (some names get opinions).
- **10 screens**: Landing Pile, Fluff Corridor, Button Room (a "get dressed" floor-button
  puzzle), Fitting Room, Sign Hall, Dust Bunny Warren, Static Floor (follow the stitches),
  Clingy Chamber, Lost Things Nook, and The Vent Door.
- **Characters**: Fortuna the crumbling fortune cookie, Mothilda the moth who keeps almost
  eating you, a Tailor Dummy, a Dust Bunny, Static Cling, and the boss, Sir Penny, a coin
  knight who is worth exactly one cent and takes it personally.
- **Battles**: FIGHT (timing bar), ACT (every enemy can be spared), ITEM, MERCY, and
  bullet-dodging with your SOLE. Save points, game over and reload, plus an ending screen
  that tallies who you spared.
