CORRECTIONS APPLYING TO ALL IMAGES
Dog: huge, naturally proportioned, long black hair; broad gentle head and heavy
Saint-Bernard-like build. Not a tri-colour mountain dog, wolf or cartoon puppy.
Cat: ordinary naturally proportioned black-and-white domestic cat.
Neither animal wears clothes, packs, saddles or invented accessories.
White: passive and scared, not a little wizard, guide or controllable third hero.
The world is stylised; natural animal anatomy and restrained fur shading must
share its lighting. No photo cutouts, human poses or sliding flat characters.

PIPELINE CAUTION
These are existing generated concept images from the conversation, not renders
of the exact P2 layout. They contain no trustworthy collision/depth data and no
separate animation layers. Do not pretend that stretching a full image across
unrelated blocks creates the finished world. A painted surface must be matched
to its actual geometry, with dynamic objects/liquids separate and depth tested.

No new illustrations are commissioned in this handoff. Inspect actual gameplay
screenshots to decide whether the implementation is approaching these targets.
