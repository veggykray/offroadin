THE ELSEWHERE — FIRST BUILD HANDOFF

This is a briefing pack, NOT a playable game.

FIRST TASK
Build only the isolated P2 “First Rise” scene: a small part of 0B, 0D and 1A.
The goal is one convincing playable visual test, not P1–P4 or the chapter.

FOR STEVE
1. Make this complete handoff ZIP accessible in the Elsewhere Codex workspace.
   For a repository-backed task, put the ZIP in the Elsewhere repository.
   Do not put this project into Slopulous or Hollow Crowns.
2. Read 01_CODEX_FIRST_PLAYABLE_PROMPT.txt, then copy its full contents into the task.
3. Codex should extract/read the pack, implement the one scene and return a
   playable ZIP with screenshots and honest testing notes. It must stop there
   for your review before building more.

CONTENTS
01_CODEX_FIRST_PLAYABLE_PROMPT.txt — the complete task, identical to the
                                  standalone prompt supplied in chat.
02_VISUAL_REFERENCE_NOTES.txt — what to preserve and what not to copy.
references/ — three existing concept PNGs from the conversation.
the_elsewhere_chapter_one_v3_1_corrected.zip — the unchanged chapter baseline.
manifest.json — included files, checksums and original reference filenames.

The original chapter archive is deliberately unchanged. This task is a small
implementation scope over that design, not Version 4 or another chapter rewrite.
Reference art is visual direction, not ready-made rigged models or a texture atlas.

The brief deliberately postpones enemies, capture/falls, potion conversion,
P1's garden opening, P3's wider settlement and P4's cross-floor detour.
