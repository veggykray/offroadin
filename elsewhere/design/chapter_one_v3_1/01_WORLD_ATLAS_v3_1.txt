THE ELSEWHERE — CHAPTER ONE: THE RIVERS ABOVE
VERSION 3.1 · DOCUMENTATION AND DIAGRAM CORRECTIONS (NOT YET A CODEX IMPLEMENTATION PROMPT)

Status: revised pre-production specification. Locations and transport are schematic; vertical links and stage logic have been checked, but in-engine fluid/AI playtests remain essential.
Target: 60–90 minutes first-time play, ~78 minutes central route including 3-minute portal opening, plus optional content.
Camera and controls: touch-first isometric game, landscape mobile and desktop tap/click.
Party: physically natural black shaggy large dog, real-looking black-and-white cat; White = small scared pale companion, non-playable.
Core: distinct floors, moving liquids, different roles in an inhabited world, stealth consequences and recovery falls.

PART ONE — THE FOUR-FLOOR WORLD ATLAS
The map coordinate grid repeats on every floor: (0,0) northwest, (2,2) southeast. Coordinates are SCHEMATIC island centroids; each island contains internal routes and subplatforms. Same X,Y across floors denotes vertical alignment. Sloping/moving transport explicitly identified.

====================================================================
FLOOR 0 — THE VERGE
====================================================================

0A — Garden Verge (grid (0, 0))
Wild garden / broken arrival portal
  World: The ordinary garden fragments persist only here: grass, a garden wall and a cracked, silent portal. A wind-herder tends strange airborne seeds.
  Residents: Whispering wind-herder, harmless; a Watcher enters briefly. The old garden bell can be rung to lure it away.
  Play: Portal chase / hide-and-divert; cat sees moving seed shadows, dog scents the frightened stranger.

0B — Refuge Sluice (grid (1, 0))
Pitted basalt floodgate and soft refuge
  World: Reed roots wrap old basalt sluicework; chipped porcelain bowls collect cyan run-off. A repairer quietly watches.
  Residents: Sluice repairer; unthreatening, not a mission-giving NPC.
  Play: Follow scent to a blocked cyan seep, scrape away reeds and turn a ceramic diverter; the resulting flow raises the float gate and forms the bridge.

0C — Catch Basin (grid (2, 0))
Terraced catch-pools and cushion reeds
  World: Soft reed-moss nets below the upper Commons catch deliberate or accidental falls. Small basin-tenders sing while sorting filters.
  Residents: Basin-tenders; they help retrieve dropped objects and never attack.
  Play: Recover by the one-way reed ramp to 0B; optional damp-reed scent clue reveals a home-memory fragment, not a new musical minigame.

0D — Root Elevator (grid (1, 1))
Spiral column and carved stone roots
  World: Stacked carved roots and a vertical ring of magenta liquid, with a readout of lift mass shown by three glowing bands.
  Residents: Tiny lift attendant takes notes but does not demand a toll.
  Play: First cat-only ascent; stronger current later lifts dog plus White. White balks once, then accepts comfort.

====================================================================
FLOOR 1 — CANAL SETTLEMENTS
====================================================================

1A — Reed Quay (grid (1, 1))
Home docks and tethered floating skiffs
  World: Working quay with basket ferries, humming reed crops, pump wheels and porcelain homes.
  Residents: Reed growers, basket porters, light-moth feeders, relaxed dock clerk.
  Play: Mass regulator at landing; gossip and signs establish how lifts and flowkeepers behave.

1B — Canal Commons (grid (2, 0))
Public crossroads over glowing conduits
  World: Lived-in village commons: bread ovens, public market steps, bridges and a Watcher who is indifferent to the animals.
  Residents: Bakers, couriers, repairers, one neutral Watcher.
  Play: Observe far-side bridge from here; fear-safe shelter and an accidental fall route.

1C — Far Pipe Yard (grid (0, 1))
Narrow maintenance sheds
  World: Thin pipework, cat-scale roofwalks, workshop tools, humming return fountain.
  Residents: Pipe stitchers and soldering flowkeepers; dog is simply too large for their roofs.
  Play: Launch cat on V03; optional route read from vibrating pipes.

1D — Hearth Courts (grid (2, 1))
Clustered ceramic cottages & mechanical yard
  World: Warm glazed homes and wheelhouse workshop. Cyan receiver on far bank, private ferry platform nearby.
  Residents: Family of Flowkeepers; gruff but kind mechanic whose skiff uses amber power.
  Play: Cat redirects cyan to bridge; later investigate why ferry no longer follows its amber courier.

====================================================================
FLOOR 2 — WORKING HEIGHTS
====================================================================

2A — Ferry Terrace (grid (1, 1))
Suspended public ferries and ticket bells
  World: Active ferry plaza with ticket bells, canopy workshops, passengers, hung lanterns and busy repairs.
  Residents: Practical ferry captain, umbrella maker, light moths stealing shiny things.
  Play: Light-moth bell mystery; captain grants access to service pipes and converter catalyst.

2B — Siphon Loft (grid (0, 1))
Vertical liquid pipe jungle / inspection deck
  World: Above an industrial void, a maze of glazed high pressure pipes and moving flowkeeper maintenance skiffs.
  Residents: Busy miniature Flowkeepers, glass baffle polishers, no enemies by default.
  Play: Cat sees pipe vibrations to find remote magenta relay; sloping return chute lands at Hearth Courts.

2C — Dye Market (grid (1, 0))
Tented dye pools, alchemical kitchens
  World: Tented dye bazaar with vats, evaporators, stove rooms, little cafés and scented ceramic workshops.
  Residents: Kindly alchemist, dyers, perfumers, nervous traders.
  Play: Convert cyan to amber while preserving a small resident supply; independent sealed amber feeder runs to upper Prism Works.

2D — Collectors’ Records (grid (2, 1))
Severe columned offices and beacon yards
  World: Collector records depot: light cones, archive shelves, pressure drains, hiding nooks, containment alcove.
  Residents: Watchers, one Collector, confused clerk, sympathetic archivist.
  Play: Observe patrol rules; use scents and lamps to route a safe escorted ascent; capture is recoverable.

====================================================================
FLOOR 3 — UPPER REACHES
====================================================================

3A — Dew Gardens (grid (2, 1))
Quiet suspended pools and translucent foliage
  World: Floating dew pools, enormous translucent leaves and caretakers growing pollinators on cyan-fed ceramic rails.
  Residents: Quiet gardeners, elder Dewkeeper, luminous moth pollinators.
  Play: Prime prism cistern; optional pollinator restoration; friendly place after hostile depot.

3B — Prism Works (grid (0, 1))
Impossible precision-machine observatory
  World: Geometric prism house with rotating glass baffles, audible resonance tubes and channels from distant floors.
  Residents: Facet polishers, spectral engineers, one bored inspector.
  Play: Solve multi-floor liquid logic by optical clues and channel routing, not simultaneous buttons.

3C — Bell Observatory (grid (1, 2))
Tall wind bells and floating pale stone
  World: Wind-chime refuge built by Bell-listeners. Its lower terrace faces a last magenta ascent to the portal dais.
  Residents: Bell-listeners and non-hostile caretakers.
  Play: Read the Echo Horn’s existing directional markings and use one relayed bark to lure the final Collector away; White finds courage to enter the roaring final lift.

3D — Sky Threshold (grid (2, 2))
Circular sky portal surrounded by fine channels
  World: Raised portal dais and single final Collector. A magenta column rises from the adjoining lower terrace.
  Residents: Ceremonial custodians; one Collector guarding portal records.
  Play: Cat silently aims the Echo Horn; dog supplies the one bark that creates the diversion and activates the final upflow. White chooses ascent.

PART FOUR — WORLD MATERIALS AND INHABITANTS
Common physical substances: modular pitted cellular basalt, milky porcelain, flexible glass reeds, copper rims, glowing cyan/magenta/amber liquids. Functional houses, fisheries, schools, workshops, baths and civic buildings SHOULD NOT be interchangeable ruined towers.
Visual contract: Animals have realistic anatomy, mass and behaviour: huge shaggy mostly black dog and standard black-and-white cat, no clothes, speech or anthropomorphic controls. Shade and simplify fur materials for cohesion with crisp, mostly flat-colour cellular-basalt/porcelain/copper world; not photographic animals pasted into a painting. Isometric realtime 3D with readable silhouettes and low visual noise at phone scale.
White: Working name of small pale frightened inhabitant; proper name deliberately withheld for story. Always capitalise.
Watcher: Small red-robed lantern patrol; reacts to White in sight, distracted by light or sound; mostly ignores cat and dog.
Collector: Large official hunter tasked with capturing White; slow search and line-of-sight pursuit, light defensive interruption only.
Archivist: Sympathetic depot clerk who quietly offers the rescue location; civilian, not guard.
Flowkeeper: Tiny maintenance inhabitant who services piping; may accidentally alter noncritical flow; not innately hostile.
Dewkeeper: Upper garden caretaker, safe and welcoming to White.
Ferry captain: Neutral-to-kind civic transporter, puzzled by White but protective after aid.
Bell-listener: Upper-floor resident who uses resonant stone for weather and signals; helps with final puzzle clues.

GEOGRAPHIC CONSISTENCY: all true lifts (V02 V03 V06 V08) have aligned shaft centroids; V04 is a marked descending gutter, V05 and V07 are mobile transports, E33 is a short local rise. F01 F02 F03 F04 align exactly; F05 is a deliberate offset fenced chute. No silent slanted vertical waterfalls.
