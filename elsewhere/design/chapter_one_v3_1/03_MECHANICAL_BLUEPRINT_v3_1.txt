CHARACTER, ENEMY, AND TOUCH RULES
  activation_vs_completion: A route needed to earn a completion flag must NOT depend on that same flag; earlier completed quests MAY unlock later routes (e.g. P6_FAVOUR or P8_ESCORTED). Local gate activation still occurs before that route is traversed.
  White_policy: White has no direct move order, follows dog when safe, waits in nearest sanctuary if dog goes to cat-only link or on dangerous route; only invited past threat after safety cue; first ascent refused once, last ascent voluntary.
  cat_dog_switch: Instant camera switch across floors; actors physically remain in their own locations, with independent move queues cleared only by new user orders.
  critical_flows: An opened route can remain visually responsive but carries a safety latch, rail, or alternate exit after first crossing; fixed reserve powers L09 from P7 onward regardless of local converter mode.
  capture: White is guided to closest defined cell annex from 2D; both animals can reach release; no random teleport. Enemy cannot deny all rescue routes.
  falls: Physical one-way descent to defined named landing. Floor drop during chase changes position, never restores previous location. All safe landings have return paths at activation stage.
  mobile: No press-and-hold interactions, complex gestures while moving, precision jumps or unpredictable physics. Tap-safe 48 CSS px targets at 780x360 landscape.

MOBILE CONTRACT
  orientation: landscape phones + desktop
  movement: tap/click walk and contextual interact, tap cat or dog portrait to switch even on different floors
  camera: auto-focus selected animal, optional floor select, pinch zoom and drag empty space to pan
  liquid: large snap-to-position directional valves/tiles; tap to select and confirm; no continuous tiny drag
  feedback: scent/whisker clues are optional short overlays, same controls on mouse and touch
  stealth: pauseable observation and forgiving telegraphing; no frame-perfect reaction
  minimum_touch_target_css_px: 48
  accessibility: shape + motion pattern reinforces colour, reduced motion, vibration optional, low-end graphics option

FUNCTIONAL LIQUID SEMANTICS
- Cyan structure: convert supplied flow to solid path only at a dedicated receiver; safety locks retain route after escort.
- Magenta motion: pressure tier (weak cat, upgraded all), moving mechanisms, and reversing/ascending columns. Visually show incoming and outgoing channels.
- Amber energy: power gates, lures, horns and ferries. Check-valve in Dye Market isolates permanent reserved P9 source from local conversion preview.
- Transform/split: local and visibly reversible. Required: convert once to open gate. Optional mastery: use bypass split to preserve kitchen coolant.
- Rule clarity: never permit a dynamic flow to dry out a necessary return route when the operating control becomes inaccessible.

ENEMY CLARITY
Watchers: lantern staff, small, reactive; Collectors: larger and active White-capturers. Civilian archivists, Flowkeepers and artisans are not enemies. White is unusually feared by most guards, not by every species. Watchers and Collectors do not attack dog or cat gratuitously. Dog can bark as one contextual distraction, nudge or block briefly, but no combat. Cats hide/climb/observe and can lure by interacting with props; no inventory of magical cat powers.

WHITE COMPANION AI
States FOLLOW, WAIT_IN_SHELTER, REFUSE_UNSAFE, PANIC_HIDE, FLEE_TO_COVER, CONFINED, REASSURED. Dog defaults as attachment; White does not teleport or instantly cross unsafe floors. He follows cat only when dog is unavailable and safe path is trivial, otherwise waits. After P2 reassurance a confidence memory is stored; at P10 he chooses lift after dog first proves it safe, with no required reassurance tap.

FALL RECOVERY
F01 1B->0C, regain floor via 0B/0D/V02. F02 2A->1A, return via 1B/1D/V05. F03 2D->1D, return via V05 and market E24. F04 3B->2B, return via V08 (cat) or E23/V07 (dog) as enabled. F05 3D->2D, return via V06/E31/E32. Fall animations safe and readable, optional recovery secret; never generic respawn at another island.
